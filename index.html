MODAK

HOW TO PLAY

YOUR GOAL

Collect Modaks and offer them to Ganesha. Collect as many as you can before the cat catches you.

CONTROLS

Use the Arrow Keys or D-Pad to move Mushika.

THE CHARACTERS

Due to our artistic limitations, the characters are represented as simple shapes:
Brown rectangle — Mushika
Gray rectangle — Cat
Yellow triangle — Modak

MODAKS

Each Modak disappears after a short time. Collect it before it disappears.

THE CAT

The cat chases Mushika when nearby. If it catches you, you lose your collected Modaks and a life. Each catch makes the cat faster.

CAT PROTECTION

Cat Protection blocks one cat attack without losing your collected Modaks.

DIFFICULTY

As your Modak count grows, Mushika gradually becomes slower. The difficulty reaches its maximum at 20 Modaks.

OFFERING

Return to Ganesha whenever you want to offer your collected Modaks.
